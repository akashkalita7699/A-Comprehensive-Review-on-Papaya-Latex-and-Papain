## A Comprehensive Review on Papaya Latex and Papain
Chemical Constituents, Biological Activities and Industrial Applications
📄 Read Full Dissertation (PDF)
#Overview
A systematic literature review examining Carica papaya latex and its principal enzyme, papain — integrating phytochemistry, biochemistry, pharmacology, and industrial biotechnology into a single evidence-based study. Completed as a B.Pharm dissertation (2026) under the Regional College of Pharmaceutical Sciences, affiliated with Srimanta Sankaradeva University of Health Sciences.
# Objectives
Evaluate the chemical composition and biochemical properties of papaya latex and papain
Synthesize evidence on biological activities — antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, wound healing, antioxidant, and anthelmintic
Survey industrial applications across food, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, textile, and biotechnology sectors
Identify gaps in current research and propose future directions
# Methodology
Systematic search across PubMed, Scopus, Google Scholar, and ScienceDirect
Literature screened using defined inclusion and exclusion criteria (1994–2024, peer-reviewed sources)
Thematic synthesis with Vancouver-style citation tracking
# Key Findings
Identified 13+ bioactive phytoconstituents across four chemical classes: cysteine proteases, alkaloids, flavonoids, and vitamins
Characterized papain's structure, catalytic mechanism, and stability profile (active across pH 3.0–9.5, thermally stable up to 80°C)
Compiled clinical and preclinical evidence across six biological activity categories
Mapped industrial applications spanning five major economic sectors
# Skills Demonstrated
Literature review methodology · Scientific writing · Data synthesis · Critical analysis · Academic referencing (Vancouver style) · Scientific presentation design
